BBC 6 Minute English (Tóm tắt lượt thoại)

Is aircon the answer to a heating world?

Bài gốc: BBC 6 Minute English YouTube: đang cập nhật
Tóm tắt lượt thoại (bản diễn đạt lại)
Bấm cụm vàng để xem phân tích
Georgie

Georgie opens by asking Neil whether he had air conditioning during the UK's recent hot summer.

Neil

Neil says he had none. He wishes he did, because the heat kept him awake.

Georgie

She had a big fan, which did not always help. Heat records keep falling worldwide, and experts expect ten new air conditioning units to be installed every second for the next 30 years. The programme asks whether aircon is the best way to cope with a warming world.

Georgie

Georgie sets a quiz: modern air conditioning was invented in 1902, but what was it first used for? Was it a) protecting people in hospitals, b) drying ink, or c) cleaning air?

Neil

Neil admits he has no idea and guesses c) cleaning air.

Georgie

She says the answer will come at the end of the programme.

Neil

Neil asks whether aircon is only about comfort.

Georgie

No. Extreme heat is a serious health risk too. It is thought that more than 30,000 people across Europe died because of this summer's heat.

Dr Radhika Khosla (BBC The Climate Question)

Dr Khosla, a climatologist, says air conditioning is life-saving. She notes that Singapore's Lee Kuan Yew linked life in the tropics to aircon, and that large parts of the world could not be inhabited without it.

Neil

Neil summarises the point: aircon is life-saving because it stops people from dying.

Georgie

Yes. Without it, many regions would be impossible to live in, which is what the word inhabit means here.

Neil

So aircon has real benefits. Is it all positive?

Georgie

No. The big downside is how much energy it uses. Dr Khosla explains the risk:

Dr Radhika Khosla (BBC The Climate Question)

If aircon becomes the first, go-to fix for extreme heat, it puts too much strain on the system. The grid could collapse and emissions would rise. This creates a vicious cycle: a warmer world leads to more aircon, which means more energy and emissions, which makes the world warmer still.

Neil

Neil explains that the grid is the network that carries electricity to towns, cities and countries. If demand is too high, you get power cuts.

Georgie

She defines a vicious cycle as a situation where a solution to a problem makes the problem even worse.

Neil

Neil asks what the solutions are.

Georgie

Dr Khosla says we should first try passive cooling, which means keeping a space comfortable without using much energy. The first step is to cut solar gain, the heat that sunlight brings into a building, mainly through shading from trees, curtains or blinds.

Neil

Neil restates it: passive cooling means cooling a building without using much energy.

Georgie

Yes. Shading blocks the sun's light and heat.

Neil

Passive cooling and shading sound simple. Is that enough?

Georgie

No. We will still need aircon, so it must be powered by sustainable energy.

Neil

Neil asks for the quiz answer.

Georgie (Quiz Result & Vocabulary Recap)

The answer is b) drying ink. Willis Carrier invented it at a printing company in Brooklyn, New York, so Neil's guess was wrong. Then they recap the vocabulary: life-saving, inhabit, the grid, vicious cycle, passive cooling and shading.

Georgie

They sign off and point listeners to the worksheet on bbclearningenglish.com to test what they have learned.

Neil

Neil says goodbye.

IELTS Speaking Part 2

Topic: Staying cool at home

01:00

Describe a way you have kept your home cool during a hot period.

You should say:

  • What you did to stay cool
  • When you started doing it and how you learned about it
  • Whether it was enough on its own, or you needed something else too
  • And explain how this approach affects energy use.

Các cụm từ nên lồng ghép vào bài nói:

# passive cooling # shading # solar gain # life-saving # vicious cycle # the grid # sustainable energy

Part 3 Discussion Questions (Thảo luận chuyên sâu):

1. Why do you think more people are installing air conditioning these days?

Xem gợi ý (thử trả lời trước nhé)

Gợi ý trả lời: Nhắc tới nhiệt độ tăng, các đợt nắng nóng ngày càng thường xuyên, và dự báo số lượng máy lạnh sẽ tăng mạnh trong những năm tới.

2. Should governments encourage passive cooling instead of air conditioning?

Xem gợi ý (thử trả lời trước nhé)

Gợi ý trả lời: Nêu lợi ích tiết kiệm điện và tránh vicious cycle; đồng thời nhìn nhận rằng máy lạnh vẫn life-saving với người già và người bệnh.

Bài mẫu tham khảo

"Last summer, my flat became so hot that I struggled to sleep for several nights. At first I only used a fan, but it was not enough."

"Then I decided to rely on passive cooling. I put up blinds and moved a plant near the window, so the shading cut the solar gain in the afternoon. The room became noticeably cooler, and I did not need to switch on the aircon as often."

"I think this habit is sensible, because it saves electricity and avoids a vicious cycle of more air conditioning and more emissions. Still, for vulnerable people, aircon can be life-saving, so I would not dismiss it completely."

Điểm cần chú ý khi trả lời:

• Diễn đạt tự nhiên: Dùng "passive cooling" hoặc "a vicious cycle" thay vì lặp lại từ chung chung như "hot" hay "cool".

• Topic-specific collocations: Dùng đúng ngữ cảnh "solar gain", "shading", "life-saving", "sustainable energy".
