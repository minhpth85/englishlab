"Having really strong social bonds increases your likelihood that you'll survive from just about anything, whether that be cancer, depression, etc. And it overall is the number one predictor of longevity. So, having strong social bonds or close relationships with other people makes you more likely to survive disease, according to Zoe.

IELTS Speaking Part 2

Topic: A difficult emotional experience

01:00

Describe a time when you or a close friend experienced emotional pain or heartbreak.

You should say:

  • When this happened and who was involved
  • How the experience physically and mentally felt
  • What you or that person did to overcome the sadness
  • And explain what you learned about human relationships from it.

Các cụm từ cần lồng ghép để đạt Band 7.5+ Lexical:

# think back to # had my heart broken # visceral pain # survival system # addicted to # number one predictor of longevity # strong social bonds

Part 3 Discussion Questions (Thảo luận chuyên sâu):

1. Why do you think romantic relationships have such a massive impact on people's mental health?

Gợi ý trả lời: Dùng các ý từ podcast: "human brains evolved for survival", "nature made us addicted to each other for protection", "changes our whole identity".

2. In modern society, are people lonelier despite being digitally connected?

Gợi ý trả lời: Nhắc tới nghiên cứu của GS Zoe Donaldson: "loneliness elevates mortality risk equivalent to smoking or obesity", "superficial online contacts vs strong real social bonds".

Model Answer Band 8.0

"If I think back to a couple of years ago, I vividly remember when my best friend had his heart broken after an abrupt break-up with his ex. At that time, it was not merely an emotional sorrow; he suffered from genuine visceral pain in his chest, as if he had broken a bone."

"From what I have learned from neuroscience, when we fall in love, our brain treats the partnership as a crucial survival system. We become deeply addicted to each other, and our brain regions are oriented completely towards that person, mirroring primal needs like hunger or thirst."

"To support him, our friend circle rallied together to reinforce strong social bonds. Medical science shows that having close social connections is the number one predictor of longevity and prevents an elevated mortality risk. Eventually, that love and connection helped him rebuild his identity and fully recover."

Tiêu chí chấm điểm giám khảo đánh giá cao:

• Coherence: Sử dụng Discourse markers để bắt đầu mượt mà (If I think back to..., From what I understand...).

• Collocations: Không lặp lại từ đơn "breakup" hay "sad", dùng thay thế bằng had one's heart broken, visceral pain, survival system, predictor of longevity.
